2. PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Create one coherent application with three main roles:

1. CITIZEN
2. RESOLVER / OFFICER
3. ADMIN

Use proper server-side role-based access control.

A citizen must never be able to access another citizen's complaints.

A resolver must only access complaints assigned to them or explicitly authorized for their resolver organization.

Admin has management and oversight permissions.

Never rely only on frontend role checks.

All important authorization must be enforced on the backend.

9. AI ANALYSIS
==================================================

After photo + description + location are available, call the configured AI service.

Do NOT fake an AI response when the AI provider is unavailable.

Return structured analysis:

{
  detected_issue,
  confidence,
  category,
  severity,
  priority,
  safety_risk,
  suggested_resolver_type,
  reasoning,
  recommended_next_action
}

Example:

Detected issue:
"Road pothole"

Confidence:
0.91

Category:
"Pothole / Road Damage"

Severity:
"HIGH"

Priority:
82

Safety risk:
"Potential accident risk"

Suggested resolver:
"Road / Municipal Maintenance"

Recommended action:
"Inspect and repair the damaged road section."

IMPORTANT:

AI is a decision-support system, not the final authority.

Show:

"AI suggestion"

and

"Citizen confirmed information"

as separate data.

If confidence is low:

"AI confidence is low. Please verify the issue and category."

If AI fails:

Do NOT block the entire complaint system.

Provide manual fallback:

- Citizen selects category
- Citizen selects severity
- Complaint can continue
- Mark AI analysis as unavailable

Never invent AI output.

Important distinction:

ACTION_TAKEN does NOT mean the complaint is finally resolved.

ACTION_TAKEN:
Resolver says physical/digital action was performed.

AWAITING_VERIFICATION:
Citizen must verify.

RESOLVED:
Citizen confirms that the problem is actually addressed.

CLOSED:
Final administrative closure.
